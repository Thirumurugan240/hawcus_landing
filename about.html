About Hawcus

Turn every lead into a conversation, and every conversation into a customer.

Hawcus is a Sales Growth System for Business. We bring your leads, sales pipeline, follow-ups, calls and WhatsApp conversations into one workspace, so your team spends its time closing deals instead of switching between tabs.

What we do

Most small and growing sales teams lose deals in the gaps between tools: a lead comes in on one channel, the follow-up happens somewhere else, the call notes live on a phone, and the pipeline is a spreadsheet nobody trusts. Hawcus closes those gaps. Leads from your ads, website and referrals land in one place, every WhatsApp chat and call is logged against the right contact, and automated follow-ups keep each conversation moving until it becomes a customer.

Why we built it

We kept seeing capable teams work hard and still let leads go cold, not because they were careless but because their tools made staying on top of every conversation almost impossible. We wanted a single system that a small team could run without a consultant, that respected how people actually sell today, and that treated fast, personal follow-up as the default rather than an afterthought. That is what Hawcus is built to do.

How we are different

  • One workspace, not five tools. Leads, pipeline, follow-ups, calls and WhatsApp live together, with a full history on every contact.
  • Built on official platforms. Hawcus connects directly to Meta's official APIs for Facebook and Instagram lead ads and the WhatsApp Business Platform, so your follow-ups stay reliable and compliant.
  • Fast to start. Most teams are up and running in minutes. Import your contacts, connect your channels, and start building your pipeline with no lengthy onboarding.
  • No lock-in. Your customer data is yours. You can export it whenever you want, and cancel any time.

Who it is for

Hawcus is for sales-led businesses that talk to their customers directly: agencies, coaching and education, real estate, travel, clinics, local services and any team that runs on leads and follow-ups. If your growth depends on replying quickly and never letting a good lead slip, Hawcus was made for you.
